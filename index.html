How NEXORA works

Whether you're hiring or selling, the process is clear from start to finish.

1

Post what you need

Sign up free and describe the work. Browse verified sellers by category or use search.

2

Pay securely into escrow

Your payment is held safely in escrow — not released until you approve the delivered work.

3

Review and approve

Message your seller, request changes if needed, then approve when you're happy. Funds release only on your say-so.

1

Apply and get verified

Set up your profile and create your gigs. Our team reviews every new seller, and your gigs go live as soon as you are verified.

2

List your services

3

Deliver and get paid

How escrow works

Your money, protected end to end

NEXORA holds your payment safely until the work is done. Here's exactly what happens.

1

Client pays

Full order amount held securely at checkout. Released only when you approve.

2

Funds held

Payment sits in escrow. The seller can see it's there — motivation to deliver.

3

Work delivered

Seller submits the completed work. You review it and request changes if needed.

4

You approve, funds release

Happy with the work? Approve it. 90% goes to the seller in 3 days. NEXORA keeps 10%.
