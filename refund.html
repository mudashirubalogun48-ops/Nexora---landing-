Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Terms of ServicePrivacy PolicyRefund Policy

The short version

  • If the seller does not deliver, you get a full refund, including the service fee.
  • If there is a problem with the work, open a dispute before you approve the delivery.
  • Once you approve a delivery, the payment is released to the seller and the order cannot be refunded through NEXORA.

1. How your money is protected

When you pay for an order, NEXORA holds the payment. The seller is only paid after you approve the delivery, or after NEXORA decides a dispute in the seller's favour. This gives you time to check the work first.

2. When you get a full refund

You will receive a full refund of the amount you paid, including the 5% service fee, if:

  • the seller does not deliver the order;
  • the seller cancels the order, or their account is closed while your order is in progress;
  • NEXORA reviews a dispute and finds the delivered work clearly does not match what the gig and your order described; or
  • you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount because of an error.

3. When a refund is not given

  • After you have approved a delivery. Approving tells us you are happy, and the payment is released to the seller.
  • When you change your mind after the seller has started the work, unless the seller agrees to cancel.
  • When the work matches the gig and your order requirements, but you simply prefer a different style. Use your included revisions for this.
  • For payments made outside NEXORA. We cannot see or protect them.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. First, message the seller on NEXORA. Many problems are fixed with a quick revision.
  2. If that does not work, go to My Orders, open the order and choose Open dispute. Explain what went wrong. Do this before you approve the delivery.
  3. NEXORA reviews the order, the messages and the delivery, and may ask both of you for details.
  4. We then release the payment to the seller, ask the seller to fix the work, or cancel the order and refund you. We will tell you which, and why.

5. How long refunds take

Once a refund is approved, we send it back to the card you paid with within 5 business days. Your bank may then take a further 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement. Refunds are made in the same currency as the original payment, so the amount shown on your statement may differ slightly if your bank converts currencies.

6. For sellers

If an order is refunded, the seller does not receive payment for it. If a seller has a pattern of undelivered or refunded orders, NEXORA may remove their verification or close their account.
