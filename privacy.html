Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run the marketplace: your account details, profile, orders and messages.
  • We never see or store your full card number. Card payments are handled by Flutterwave.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXORA ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you use our marketplace. It applies to buyers, sellers and visitors.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored in scrambled form, never readable by us), name and whether you are a buyer or seller.
  • Profile details: profile photo, headline, bio, skills and languages that you choose to add. Seller profiles are visible to the public.
  • Gigs: titles, descriptions, prices, images and other details sellers publish.
  • Orders: what was ordered, prices, fees, order status, requirements, delivery notes, dispute reasons and dates.
  • Messages: messages you send to other users through NEXORA.
  • Payment records: payment references and status from our payment provider. We do not receive your full card number or security code.
  • Verification details: information sellers provide so we can review them.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends, such as device type and IP address, which our hosting and database providers record for security.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show gigs and seller profiles, take orders, hold and release payments, and pay sellers.
  • To let buyers and sellers message each other.
  • To review sellers, investigate disputes, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send important emails, such as account confirmation, password resets and order updates.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run NEXORA, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase: stores our database, files and sign-in system.
  • Flutterwave: processes card payments.
  • Brevo: sends our account emails.
  • Netlify: hosts the website.

Other users see what is needed to do business together. For example, a seller sees a buyer's name, order requirements and messages, and anyone can see a seller's public profile and gigs. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

5. Cookies and browser storage

NEXORA uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember an order while you check out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you clear your browser's storage, you will be signed out.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Order and payment records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax, disputes and fraud prevention, even after an account is closed. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Keeping it safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure users can only see their own orders and messages, and only NEXORA staff can review disputes and verifications. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile at any time from your account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing us. We will reply within 30 days.
  • Some records, such as completed orders and payments, may need to be kept for legal reasons even after you ask for deletion. We will tell you if this applies.

9. Children

NEXORA is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NEXORA changes. If the changes are important, we will tell you on the site or by email.
